Ten more reveals for a state occasion

These follow the same rules as the first six: 8 to 10 seconds of slow, deliberate motion, no party effects, the full name and titles set large, and sound with weight. They draw on Sabah's land and sea, the language of formal ceremony, and the modern spectacle that large state events now use.

Key frame · the swarm holds the name over the bay

Drone Constellation

Recommended

Hundreds of lights rise over a night skyline by the water, the kind of drone show now used at national celebrations.

  1. 0–3 sLights lift off the waterfront in waves and gather into a slowly turning ring.
  2. 3–6 sThe ring folds into the outline of Mount Kinabalu, then into the shape of Sabah.
  3. 6–9 sThe swarm flows into the winner's name, which holds over the water with its reflection rippling below.
Built with Three.js instanced points + GSAP (shape morphing)Length ~9 s

Modern and grand, and it reuses the real Sabah outline from Map of Light. It's an animation, so no drones or flight permits are involved.

Key frame · the page settles and the initial is gilded

Book of Honour

Recommended

A great leather-bound book lies open on a lectern under a reading lamp, its pages filled with every entrant's name.

  1. 0–4 sPages turn by themselves, quickly at first, then each one more slowly, with names visible in fine script.
  2. 4–7 sThe last page settles flat. One line glows as gold leaf is laid into its first letter, in the style of an illuminated manuscript.
  3. 7–9 sThe camera moves down to the page and the winner's name is drawn in ink beside the gilded initial.
Built with Three.js page-curl meshes + GSAPLength ~9 s

Quiet and dignified. Turning pages full of real names shows that everyone was in the draw.

Key frame · light gathers in the blue

Sipadan Deep

Recommended

A slow descent into the deep blue off Sipadan, one of the world's great dive sites. Calm, luminous and wholly Sabahan.

  1. 0–3 sThe camera slips beneath the surface. Shafts of sunlight sway and fine particles drift past.
  2. 3–6 sA green turtle glides slowly across the frame, and a column of silver bubbles rises behind it carrying the names upward.
  3. 6–9 sThe bubbles gather and settle into soft, glowing letters that form the winner's name, held in the light from above.
Built with Three.js (caustics shader, light shafts) + GSAPLength ~9 s

Shows visiting delegations Sabah's marine heritage. The turtle is stylised; we'd check its shape against the green and hawksbill turtles found at Sipadan.

Key frame · the star opens its centre

Golden Geometry

An eight-pointed star pattern drawn in fine gold lines, in the geometric language of Malaysia's formal architecture and state halls.

  1. 0–3 sLines draw themselves from the centre with compass-and-ruler precision, forming interlocking stars.
  2. 3–6 sThe pattern rotates in slow layers and spreads outward to tile the screen in deep green and gold.
  3. 6–9 sThe central star opens like a gate and the winner's name is set inside it.
Built with SVG/Canvas line drawing + GSAPLength ~9 s

Purely geometric, with no script or religious text. Formal and timeless, and it suits a government hall.

Key frame · the doors stand open

The Grand Doors

A pair of tall carved timber doors in a darkened hall, the classic image of an audience being granted.

  1. 0–3 sThe camera approaches the doors. A thin line of light shows where they meet.
  2. 3–6 sThe doors swing slowly open on heavy hinges, light pours across the floor and dust drifts in the beam.
  3. 6–9 sThe winner's name stands in the doorway, set in light and casting a long shadow toward the audience.
Built with Three.js (carved panels, volumetric light) + GSAPLength ~9 s

Simple and immediately understood by any audience. The carving can follow a Sabahan timber style, or the venue's own doors if you have photos.

Key frame · the bloom is fully open

Kinabalu Orchid

A rare slipper orchid of Mount Kinabalu, drawn in fine gold wire, opens petal by petal.

  1. 0–3 sA single stem rises from darkness and a closed bud forms at its tip in gold filigree.
  2. 3–6 sThe petals unfold one by one: the upright sepal, then the long side petals reaching outward, then the pouch.
  3. 6–9 sA soft light gathers in the pouch and spills down the stem, and the winner's name appears below the flower.
Built with SVG line drawing + GSAP (DrawSVG-style)Length ~9 s

Graceful, and fitting for Sabah's natural heritage. The form follows Rothschild's slipper orchid, which grows only around Kinabalu; we'd draw it from botanical references.

Key frame · the centre standard has unfurled

Silk Standards

A row of tall silk banners hangs from a gilded rail, like the standards in a ceremonial hall.

  1. 0–3 sThe banners hang still. A breath of air moves through them, and names woven into the silk catch the light.
  2. 3–6 sThe side banners part and draw back as the centre standard drops and unfurls in one long, heavy ripple.
  3. 6–9 sGold thread stitches the winner's name across the silk, and the cloth settles with a final soft sway.
Built with Three.js cloth shader + GSAPLength ~9 s

Rich and formal. The colours can match the event's livery; we avoid national flag colourways to keep it neutral before foreign guests.

Key frame · the last grains fall

Hourglass of Gold

A tall brass-and-glass hourglass, lit from the side, turns once and lets its gold sand run.

  1. 0–2 sThe hourglass turns over slowly on its pivot and the sand begins to fall in a fine stream.
  2. 2–6 sThe camera follows the stream. Names flicker in the falling grains and the lower bulb fills.
  3. 6–9 sThe last grains fall. The sand in the lower bulb lifts and drifts out through the glass to settle as the winner's name beside it.
Built with Three.js glass + particle sand + GSAPLength ~9 s

A timeless symbol of the moment arriving. It works well for the final draw of the night.

Key frame · the name on the water curtain

Fountain of Light

A night fountain show: jets of water lit in gold and white, choreographed to rise and fall in time with the music.

  1. 0–3 sLow jets ripple across a dark pool in a slow wave from left to right.
  2. 3–6 sThe tall jets rise in sequence, arcs cross overhead, and the spray catches the light.
  3. 6–9 sA sheer curtain of water rises in the centre and the winner's name is projected onto it, shimmering as the water falls.
Built with Three.js particles + GSAPLength ~9 s

Spectacular on a big LED wall and familiar from national celebrations. Best for outdoor or evening programmes.

Key frame · above the canopy at first light

Rainforest Ascent

The camera rises through the layers of Borneo's ancient rainforest, from the dark forest floor to the open sky.

  1. 0–3 sFerns and roots in green shadow. Light filters down and the sound of the forest is low and close.
  2. 3–6 sThe camera climbs past giant trunks and through layers of leaves, which part as it passes, into drifting mist.
  3. 6–9 sIt breaks above the canopy into a soft morning sky over the Danum and Maliau forests, and the name appears over the mist.
Built with Three.js layered foliage + fog + GSAPLength ~10 s

A message about Sabah's conservation story that speaks to international guests. It pairs naturally with Kinabalu Dawn.

Suggested next builds

  • Start with: Drone Constellation, Book of Honour and Sipadan Deep. They are the three most distinct from the designs already built.
  • Easy wins: Golden Geometry and Kinabalu Orchid are mostly 2D line drawing, so they are quick to build and very light to run.
  • Needs input from you: photos of the venue's doors (for The Grand Doors, optional) and the event's colours (for Silk Standards).
  • All ten would use the same names list, fair draw, winners log and clicker controls as the rest of the studio, and run fully offline.
